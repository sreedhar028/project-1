# Online Retail Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business intelligence dashboard designed for online retail executives. The dashboard visualizes key performance indicators and sales trends to support data-driven decision making.

## Dashboard Components

### Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Revenue**: $9,747,750
- **Product Variety**: 4,000 unique stock codes in inventory
- **Transaction Volume**: 21,000 unique invoice numbers processed

### Revenue Analysis

#### Monthly Revenue Trend
The dashboard tracks monthly revenue performance, revealing:
- Strong year-end performance with November ($1,462K) as the peak month
- Consistent growth trajectory from January ($560K) to December ($1,183K)
- Seasonal patterns with minor dip in April ($493K) after March ($683K)

#### Day of Week Analysis
Sales distribution across weekdays shows:
- Saturday generates highest revenue ($2,121.9K)
- Weekdays (Tuesday-Friday) consistently outperform early week days
- Monday has the lowest performance ($123.77K)

#### Geographic Market Analysis
The dashboard segments markets by performance:

**Top 5 Markets:**
1. United Kingdom: $8,188K
2. Netherlands: $285K
3. Ireland: $263K
4. Germany: $222K
5. France: $197K

**Bottom 5 Markets:**
1. Saudi Arabia: $0.13K
2. Bahrain: $0.55K
3. Czech Republic: $0.71K
4. RSA (South Africa): $1K
5. Brazil: $1.14K

### Year-over-Year Performance

## Executive Summary

This repository contains a comprehensive business intelligence solution designed for online retail Chief Marketing Officers (CMOs) and executive teams. The multi-view dashboard provides actionable insights into customer behavior, product performance, and sales patterns across time and geography.


## 📊 Dashboard Components

### Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Count of Quantity | 540K | Total number of items ordered |
| Sum of Quantity | 5,190K | Total volume of units sold |
| Average of Quantity | 9.60 | Average units per order |

### Product Performance Analysis

The dashboard highlights top-performing products:

1. **JUMBO BAG RED RETROSPOT**: 47K units
2. **WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER**: 35K units
3. **PARTY BUNTING**: 18K units
4. **REGENCY CAKESTAND**: 13K units
5. **DOTCOM POSTAGE**: 1K units

These five products represent approximately 22% of total quantity sold, highlighting the importance of key SKUs in the product portfolio.

### Customer Geographic Distribution

#### Top 5 Countries by Order Quantity
1. **United Kingdom**: 4,277K units (82% of total)
2. **Netherlands**: 200K units
3. **Ireland (EIRE)**: 143K units
4. **Germany**: 117K units
5. **France**: 110K units

The interactive world map visualization allows drilling into specific regions to identify customer clusters and expansion opportunities.

### Temporal Analysis

#### Hourly Distribution of Orders
- **Peak Hours**: 10AM-3PM (10-15 hours)
- **Highest Volume**: 12PM (683K units)
- **Business Hours (9AM-5PM)**: Represent over 75% of daily volume
- **Early Morning (6-8AM)**: Minimal activity
- **Evening (18-20PM)**: Declining but notable volume

#### Daily Pattern
- **Highest Volume Days**: Saturday (1,142K) and Friday (984K)
- **Weekday Growth**: Progressive increase Tuesday through Friday
- **Lowest Volume**: Monday (67K) shows significant drop from weekend

#### Monthly Trend
- **Peak Season**: November (739K) represents seasonal holiday shopping
- **Strong Q4**: October-December shows highest sustained performance
- **Low Season**: February (277K) and April (293K)
- **Year Pattern**: Clear upward trajectory with Q4 peak followed by December slight decline

## 💡 Business Insights

### Product Strategy
- The top 5 products represent significant volume concentration, suggesting opportunities for:
  - Bundling complementary items with top sellers
  - Creating variations of successful product designs
  - Focusing promotional activities on high-volume SKUs

### Geographic Focus
- With over 80% of volume from the UK market:
  - Opportunity to deepen penetration in secondary European markets
  - Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, and France show promising adoption
  - Consider localized marketing campaigns for these secondary markets

### Timing Optimization
- Clear patterns in hourly, daily, and monthly distribution suggest:
  - Concentrating customer service staff during 10AM-3PM peak hours
  - Planning promotional campaigns for mid-week to boost Monday performance
  - Preparing inventory and operations for Q4 seasonal surge
  - Creating targeted offers during February-April low season
